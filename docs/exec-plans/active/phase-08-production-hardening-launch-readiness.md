# Phase 08 — Production Hardening and Launch Readiness

## Status

In progress. Phase 07 is fully implemented, live verified, independently documented, archived, pushed, and synchronized at remote divergence `0/0`.

## Objective

Prove the approved Mariner Nexus production experience is reliable, fast, accessible, secure, SEO-complete, operationally safe, browser-compatible, and launch-ready under strict feature freeze.

## Governing rule

Fix, verify, optimize, launch. Do not invent.

## In scope

1. Audit and repair launch-critical performance, accessibility, responsiveness, browser compatibility, SEO, metadata, social sharing, analytics, security, inquiry safety, content, links, redirects, errors, dependencies, source hygiene, and Vercel configuration.
2. Verify the complete public route and user-journey matrix without unnecessary Production inquiry submissions.
3. Establish measured performance and client-JavaScript baselines.
4. Produce a Vercel Preview release candidate and final review captures.
5. Document an executable launch checklist, rollback plan, final completion report, and GO / GO WITH DOCUMENTED NON-BLOCKER / NO-GO recommendation.

## Out of scope

No new page, section, service, case study, signature interaction, visual or animation system, acquisition feature, integration, analytics product, 3D technology, AI capability, CMS, database, authentication, or marketing feature. Non-critical enhancements go to the post-launch backlog and are not implemented. Production launch and DNS changes require separate approval.

## Acceptance

- Critical routes, journeys, redirects, unauthorized-project protections, inquiry behavior, and external links pass.
- Automated and manual accessibility, keyboard, semantic, contrast, and reduced-motion review pass.
- Desktop, tablet, mobile, rotation, and the Chrome/Safari/Firefox/Edge matrix are acceptable with no horizontal overflow.
- Core Web Vitals and route/client/image/font loading are acceptable and documented.
- SEO, sitemap, robots, canonical metadata, OpenGraph, favicon, analytics, legal copy, public claims, and launch copy pass.
- Secrets remain server-only and absent from the repository and Git history; dependencies and build pass.
- Vercel configuration is safely audited without exposing values or mutating protected inquiry infrastructure.
- The exact release candidate is deployed to Preview, reviewed, and paired with rollback instructions.
- No unapproved feature is introduced.

## Validation

Run type generation, typecheck, lint, tests, production build, dependency/security audit, secret/history scan, route/link/redirect checks, bundle and asset measurement, browser and device review, keyboard and reduced-motion review, accessibility automation plus manual semantics, representative performance measurement, inquiry failure-path checks, and Vercel Preview inspection.

## Stop condition

Provide the Phase 08 completion report, release-candidate URL, review evidence, launch checklist, rollback plan, material decision record, and launch recommendation. Do not promote or launch Production. Stop for final launch approval.
