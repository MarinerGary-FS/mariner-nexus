## Identity DNA carried forward

EA-05 uses Mariner identity through architectural convergence, a controlled vertical axis, calm dark materiality, precise spacing, and Mineral Teal only where the system is active. The approved metallic micro-mark now becomes the peak's material center rather than wallpaper: its blue energy is visually subordinated through desaturation while a single Mineral Teal convergence signal retains semantic authority. It does not use generic particles, decorative orbiting, or unexplained spectacle. The identity mark becomes both asset and structural principle: multiple capabilities resolve through it into one useful organizational response.

## Source boundary

No dormant client asset is used. Undugu remains the only authorized public client, and EA-05 does not import Undugu identity into the Mariner-owned Nexus peak.
