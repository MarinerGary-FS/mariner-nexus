## Verified production and repository evidence

### Starting condition

Gary confirmed that this was not a greenfield project. The previous website primarily functioned as a portfolio for Gary's background acting work. It served that earlier objective, but became strategically obsolete as his ownership of Mariner Nexus, commercial real-estate work, business and technology operations, networking, and community relationships expanded beyond what the acting-focused portfolio could represent.

### Objective

Verified current objective: create an immersive executive intelligence environment that establishes Gary's strategic worldview, demonstrates translation between vision and execution, builds executive trust and curiosity, routes visitors through a connected initiative ecosystem, and ends in a controlled invitation rather than a hard sell.

### Audience

Verified audiences include founders, business owners, operators, organizational decision-makers, executive or hiring leaders, strategic partners, community organizations, and aligned speaking or consulting opportunities. The experience assumes a visitor evaluating Gary's thinking and fit, not shopping a commodity service list.

### Personal-brand strategy

GaryMariner.com is the personal executive and philosophical layer of the ecosystem. It gives one individual a coherent digital presence by organizing worldview, expertise, evidence, initiatives, public voice, and contact pathways around Gary rather than around a corporate service catalog. Mariner Nexus is the execution layer; Undugu is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization and the ecosystem's human/community layer; Black Relativity is the public-intelligence layer.

### Information architecture

The production homepage uses six ordered scenes: Entry into the system, The translator, Systems and infrastructure, Human-centered transformation, The ecosystem, and Controlled invitation. Supporting architecture includes `/contact`, `/social`, and a private/noindex `/network` gateway. Legacy `/about`, `/services`, and `/case-studies` paths redirect into Mariner Nexus rather than duplicating corporate depth.

### Experience strategy

The experience is a dark, editorial, cinematic environment using IBM Plex Sans and Newsreader, executive portraiture, numbered chapters, low-density opening copy, structured translation pairs, restrained environmental motion, ecosystem nodes, and a controlled invitation. It is intentionally neither a résumé, influencer profile, generic consultant site, nor portfolio grid.

### Responsive behavior

Live production was inspected at 1440×1000, 834×1000, and 390×844. All six scenes remain present, typography and content retain parity, desktop uses scale while mobile uses focused intimacy, the mobile navigation becomes a full-width action surface, and all tested routes reported zero document-level horizontal overflow. `/contact` and `/social` also reported zero mobile overflow. Touch-oriented primary actions measured at least 44px high; small footer text links remain secondary navigation.

### Systems actually delivered

Verified delivered systems are:

- statically prerendered Next.js App Router pages;
- a Cal.com embed and configured strategy/systems/optimization meeting paths;
- a labeled contact form that prepares a local `mailto:` message rather than persisting data to a backend;
- conditional Google Analytics event emission through `gtag` when available;
- secure external routing to Mariner Nexus, Undugu, and LinkedIn using new-tab isolation;
- an ecosystem gateway and standalone social command surface;
- a downloadable vCard/contact system and private `/network` gateway;
- redirect routing from superseded personal-site service/about/case-study paths into Mariner Nexus;
- semantic server content, reduced-motion CSS, and responsive layout behavior.

No database, CRM persistence, server-side form handler, custom authentication, or verified production automation beyond those visible relationships should be claimed.

The live footer publicly verifies “Built by Mariner Nexus,” the repository provides direct implementation evidence, and Gary's supplied transformation record authorizes Mariner's strategic repositioning, personal digital identity architecture, information architecture, experience design, UX/UI, responsive engineering, and ecosystem-routing role.
