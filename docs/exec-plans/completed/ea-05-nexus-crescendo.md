# Experience Amplification 05 — The Nexus Crescendo

## Phase objective

Turn the existing explanatory Nexus into the homepage's experiential and intellectual peak: one live architecture that reorganizes around Build, Transform, Connect, and Explore while preserving Strategy, Experience, Systems, and a human or organizational outcome.

## Source of truth

- Canonical doctrine in `docs/mariner-nexus-redesign/`.
- DEC-007, DEC-008, DEC-009, DEC-030, DEC-031, DEC-032, DEC-038, DEC-040.
- Approved EA-01 through EA-04 completion reports.
- User-authorized EA-05 brief.

## In scope

- Required graphics-architecture evaluation and identity-asset audit.
- Minimal homepage continuity compression between Systems and Nexus.
- A materially amplified Nexus with four unchanged objectives.
- Semantic DOM, advanced decorative SVG, CSS spatial depth, deliberate user-controlled reorganization, and an unmistakable dark settled peak.
- Independent desktop, tablet, mobile, reduced-motion, keyboard, screen-reader, and JavaScript-free behavior.
- Expanded dynamic content-integrity audit, review evidence, performance comparison, decisions, and completion report.

## Out of scope

- EA-06 release/reassurance, final conversion redesign, additional clients, inquiry changes, Production deployment, unrelated homepage sections, WebGL/Three.js/Canvas, new graphics packages, CMS, database, or AI capability.

## Required experience

The preceding operational flow must feel as though it enters the Nexus rather than ending before it. On entry, a restrained core expands into a legible architecture. Deliberate objective selection changes topology, relationships, context, and outcome—not merely copy. Once controls are active, the system never autoplays. The final viewport remains a dark, controlled peak without a white release or CTA.

## Engineering requirements

- Semantic HTML is authoritative; graphics are decorative.
- Keep a stable union of nodes mounted for focus and perceptual continuity.
- One bounded client island owns objective/node selection and a one-time entry acknowledgment.
- No scroll trap, global listener, per-frame render, layout-measurement loop, network request, or new dependency.
- Rapid switching must cancel stale settling state.
- Reduced motion resolves changes immediately.
- JavaScript-free output must expose the complete four-objective model.

## Responsive requirements

- Desktop: full spatial field with differentiated topology and depth.
- Tablet: intentional grouped architecture rather than compressed desktop.
- Mobile: a strong layered vertical architecture, not a tiny graph or generic accordion.

## Acceptance gates

Engineering, Meaning, Impact, Crescendo, Coherence, Mariner Identity, Content Integrity, dynamic collision, accessibility, performance, and responsive validation must pass. Stop before EA-06.
