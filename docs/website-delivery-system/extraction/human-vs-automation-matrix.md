# Human Versus Automation Matrix

Automation produces evidence. Accountable humans decide meaning, authorization, tradeoffs, and release.

| Activity | Automation may | Human must | Gate/authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intake | Normalize answers, flag omissions | Interpret context and ambiguity | Truth gate |
| Truth | Track status/evidence, block ineligible claims | Verify evidence and approve client-provided facts | Client/strategic lead |
| Strategy | Compare profile against playbooks | Choose positioning, audience priority, and success model | Strategic fit |
| Scope/tier | Calculate indicators and recommend | Set tier, risk modifier, exclusions, and budget | Client + delivery lead |
| IA/content | Check completeness and route consistency | Shape hierarchy, narrative, and voice | Meaning gate |
| Visual identity | Validate tokens/contrast | Judge fidelity, distinctiveness, restraint | Identity review |
| Responsive | Sweep widths/states and capture evidence | Judge hierarchy, rhythm, and usability | Content Integrity |
| Accessibility | Run semantic/contrast/focus checks | Review comprehension, reading order, motion impact | Accessibility gate |
| Engineering | Typecheck, lint, build, test, audit | Approve architecture and exceptions | Engineering gate |
| Advanced experience | Measure weight/performance and fallbacks | Decide whether it communicates enough to exist | Impact gate |
| Integrations | Exercise contracts/failures in safe environments | Approve data/vendor/operational consequences | Risk/owner gate |
| Cost | Surface recurring or metered services | Explicitly authorize new material cost | Owner-Protected |
| Preview | Run route/link/metadata/QA manifests | Accept experience and truthful public presentation | Client/internal approval |
| Production | Execute approved immutable promotion and smoke | Authorize promotion or rollback | Owner-Protected |
| Incident | Gather logs and test rollback health | Decide rollback when policy threshold is met | Release owner |
| Handoff | Generate inventory and evidence bundle | Confirm ownership, access, training, and acceptance | Client + delivery lead |

## Automation boundaries

Codex may perform reversible, scoped implementation and validation that an approved plan authorizes. It must not invent historical facts, approve its own public claims, silently change scope, buy services, expose secrets, promote Production, or treat a green automated suite as visual/strategic approval.
