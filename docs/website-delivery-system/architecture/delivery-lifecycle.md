# Delivery Lifecycle

Truth, change control, evidence capture, and decision recording run through every stage.

| Stage | Purpose | Required exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Classify and authorize | Confirm engagement, authority, constraints, and allowed public use | Authorization and owner map |
| 1. Intake and initial truth | Capture knowns, unknowns, sources, risks, and required input | Initial profile and truth ledger |
| 2. Discovery and evidence | Understand business, audience, content, operations, and current experience | Evidence inventory and findings |
| 3. Strategy, scope, and success | Define objectives, tier, risk, exclusions, success, and approvals | Approved strategic brief |
| 4. Experience and content architecture | Define journeys, IA, content model, conversion, and responsive intent | Approved structural specification |
| 5. Identity translation | Translate identity into project-specific digital rules | Identity profile and fidelity review |
| 6. Technical and operational architecture | Select stack, data, integrations, environments, security, and ownership | Architecture and operating decisions |
| 7. Foundation | Establish semantic primitives, tokens, metadata, routing, and QA hooks | Engineering-gate evidence |
| 8. Core implementation | Build complete meaningful routes and workflows | Functional Preview |
| 9. Conditional amplification and integrations | Add only justified interaction, graphics, and external systems | Impact/integration evidence and fallbacks |
| 10. Integrity, QA, review, and revision | Run automated gates and human desktop/tablet/mobile review | Approved QA dossier and resolved exceptions |
| 11. Release, promotion, smoke, and handoff | Certify accumulated system, promote approved candidate, verify, transfer operations | Release record, rollback target, smoke and handoff |

Stages may compress for lower-complexity work, but their obligations do not disappear. A stage may be explicitly not applicable with rationale; silence is not completion.

## Change classes

- **Correction:** restores truth or approved behavior; record cause and validation.
- **Content Revision:** changes approved language or assets without changing strategy; re-run truth, content, and affected visual checks.
- **Design Revision:** changes approved composition or expression within scope; re-run meaning, responsive, accessibility, and affected impact checks.
- **Scope Expansion:** adds deliverables, cost, timeline, maintenance, or obligations; reapprove scope and tier/risk.
- **Architecture Change:** alters system boundaries, providers, data, or durable doctrine; create a decision record and reapprove engineering/operations.
- **Post-Approval Change:** any change after a relevant approval or release freeze; invalidate that approval and recertify the affected and accumulated system.

Changes can move work backward to the earliest affected stage. Automation must not self-classify a consequential change downward.
