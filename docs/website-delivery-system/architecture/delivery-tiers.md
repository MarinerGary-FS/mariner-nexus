# Delivery Tiers and Risk

Tiers control process depth, not care, accessibility, truth, or visual quality.

| Tier | Typical scope | Added rigor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Essential Presence | Focused marketing or personal presence, limited integrations | Lean discovery, complete baseline gates, proportionate review |
| 2 — Strategic Experience | Substantial positioning, content architecture, identity, interaction, or conversion | Deeper strategy/content cycles and experience evidence |
| 3 — Connected Experience | Forms, workflows, structured data, external services, or operational ownership | Integration contracts, failure simulation, data/security and operations review |
| 4 — Signature System | Bespoke architecture, advanced experience, high stakes, or complex delivery | Formal impact budgets, wider certification, rollback rehearsal, accumulated-system review |

## Independent risk modifier

Set **Low, Moderate, High, or Critical** from business criticality, public exposure, data sensitivity, integrations, stakeholders, legal/regulatory sensitivity, interaction complexity, content volume, timeline, and maintenance. Risk can elevate gates without changing visual ambition or commercial tier.

## Independent amplification level

- **A0 Functional:** meaning relies on content, typography, and composition.
- **A1 Refined:** modest transitions and contextual polish.
- **A2 Expressive:** distinctive authored motion or environmental moments.
- **A3 Immersive:** sustained choreography or advanced visual systems.
- **A4 Signature:** bespoke interaction/graphics central to the proposition.

Amplification requires a meaning statement, asset readiness, accessibility/reduced-motion behavior, performance budget, maintenance owner, and budget approval. A Tier 1 project can be visually excellent at A0; a Tier 4 system does not automatically earn A4.

Pricing and maintenance packages remain **Required Input**; WDS-01 does not infer them.

## Commercial boundary

Delivery tiers do not map to public fixed prices. The canonical commercial sequence is Inquiry → Discovery → Requirements → Architecture / Scope → Quote. Internal estimation may be considered later, but commercial pricing is excluded from the Project Kernel.
