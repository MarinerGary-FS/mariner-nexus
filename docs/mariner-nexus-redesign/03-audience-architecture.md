# 03 — Audience Architecture

## Client states

### CREATE
Establishing something new. The client may have vision but little digital structure.

Message:
> You do not need to arrive with the solution figured out.

### EVOLVE
Existing digital presence no longer represents the quality, ambition, or direction of the organization/individual.

Case studies are especially valuable here.

### TRANSFORM
Experience and operational systems are fragmented and need to work together.

Message:
> We think beyond the screen.

## Decision roles

### Founder / Individual
Needs Mariner Nexus to bring words and vision to digital life.

### Business Owner
Evaluates credibility, customer experience, time, cost, and business utility.

### Executive / Organizational Leader
Evaluates risk, process, execution quality, support burden, and operational fit.

### Internal Champion / Researcher
May create an opportunity before Mariner Nexus knows it exists. Needs strong material they can share internally and use to advocate for the engagement.

## Anti-persona

Commodity buyer primarily seeking the cheapest page-count/template solution.

Do not design the experience around this buyer.

## Principles

> Meet the client at the level of the problem, not the level of their technical knowledge.

> Our clients don't need to speak technology. They need to communicate their vision. Mariner Nexus translates that vision into a digital experience.

> Technical sophistication should be experienced before it needs to be explained.

> Show where the client started, what needed to change, and what became possible.

## Emotional objective

Trust → Curiosity → Recognition → Surprise → Confidence → Possibility → Action.

The lasting thought:

> What could Mariner Nexus do with mine?
