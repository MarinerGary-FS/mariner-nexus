# Website Delivery Approval Gates

## Gate A — Positioning
Can a visitor understand what the client is, who it serves, and why it matters?

## Gate B — Information Architecture
Can a first-time visitor find the information and actions that matter without learning the site's structure?

## Gate C — Experience
Does the digital experience reflect the client's actual ambition and identity rather than a reusable studio aesthetic?

## Gate D — Systems
Does every meaningful interaction have a clear post-action flow?

## Gate E — Responsive
Does mobile have its own choreography rather than a compressed desktop composition?

## Gate F — Accessibility
Can keyboard, reduced-motion, assistive, and touch users reach the same information and actions?

## Gate G — Performance
Does the experience feel immediate? Advanced effects may not block content, navigation, or interaction.

## Gate H — Production
Are analytics, metadata, error states, external links, forms, deployment configuration, and launch checks complete?
